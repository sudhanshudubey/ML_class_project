Interpretation limit

Which way does the relationship run?

Job seekers report higher training and network scores. Their situation may lead them to train or use their networks; those experiences may also precede the job search.

This survey cannot establish the sequence or isolate a causal effect. The scores include past experiences and perceptions, rather than direct measures of activity frequency.

Methods, meaning & limitations

The reasoning behind the results.

The dashboard presents the completed course analysis. Its conclusions apply to the observed survey sample and the model evaluations shown here.

From survey to interpretation

01

Explore the respondents and twelve scores

Frequency tables, score summaries, histograms and boxplots describe the sample. Pearson correlations, ANOVA with η² and chi-square with Cramér’s V describe relationships.

02

Prepare the modelling variables

Remove the respondent identifier. Combine the two smallest sectors with Other. Group salary into < 60k, 60–99k and > 99k. Dummy encode categorical variables with one reference category each. Retain all 460 respondents.

03

Explain self-efficacy

Fit linear regression with the eleven other scores and personal variables. Compare it with a scores-only model, using fitted RMSE, R² and adjusted R². Interpret raw and standardised coefficients together.

04

Evaluate job-seeker classification

Fit logistic regression on the stratified training split. Evaluate validation accuracy against the majority-class baseline, inspect the confusion matrix and job-seeker recall, and interpret odds ratios.

What the analysis cannot establish

  • The convenience sample is not representative of all senior executives.
  • Self-reported scores describe perceptions of employability.
  • Correlated predictors make coefficient rankings conditional on the model.
  • Sparse categories and a small validation job-seeker group limit precision.
  • Associations do not determine causal direction.

Restricted course source

The survey dataset is restricted to course use and is excluded from this public project. This dashboard contains aggregate summaries and model statistics only; it does not contain respondent records or load the original CSV.

For HR & outplacement teams

Use the findings to support a conversation.

Separate competence from confidence

Discuss achievements, transferable skills and realistic career options. Flexibility and value awareness are relevant topics, while the survey does not demonstrate the effect of a coaching intervention.

Assess each person’s support needs

Review training needs and how professional contacts are used. Establish employment status directly. Further evaluation would be needed before using this classifier operationally.
