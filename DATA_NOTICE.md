# Data source and publication notice

The analysis uses an anonymised survey supplied for the Introduction to Machine Learning course at emlyon business school. The course brief restricts the dataset to course use and says not to share or publish it.

**The dataset is not included in this public repository.** The public notebook contains analysis code and written observations, with all saved cell outputs removed. The dashboard contains selected aggregate summaries and model statistics, without respondent records. It does not load, embed or offer a download of the original CSV.

This repository does not grant access to the survey data or any redistribution rights. Rerunning the analysis requires separately authorised access to the original dataset. Lecture materials, the course brief and the submission report are also excluded.

## Survey source supplied in the course

Deacken, N., & Jaotombo, F. (2019). Etat des lieux sur les mesures en variables latentes : le cas du sentiment d'employabilité des cadres séniors du secteur privé français. *Revue internationale de psychosociologie et de gestion des comportements organisationnels, XXV*(62), 53–82.

No open-data licence is asserted for the survey dataset.
